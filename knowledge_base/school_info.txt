School Office Hours:
The school office is open from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM every weekday.

School Uniform:
Students are expected to wear the school uniform every day.

Admissions:
Admissions are handled through the school office.

Lunch Menu:
Today's lunch includes grilled chicken, steamed vegetables, rice, and fruit. Menus change daily; check the notice board for weekly menus.

Contact Information:
Main Office: +1-555-151-0041
Email: office@school.edu

Library Hours:
The library is open from 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM on school days.

School Address and Parking:
123 Main St. Use the Oak Ave entrance. Visitor parking is available in the north lot.

Emergency Procedures:
In case of emergency, follow the school's evacuation plan and contact the main office immediately. For medical emergencies call 911.

Counseling Services:
Student counseling is available by appointment through the guidance office.

Transportation / Bus Routes:
Bus routes and pickup times are posted on the school's transportation board and on the website.

Extracurriculars:
Clubs and sports schedules are posted at the start of each term; contact the activities coordinator for details.

Tuition & Payments:
Tuition invoices are sent at the start of each term; payments can be made online or at the school's finance office.
 
School Holidays & Calendar:
The academic calendar and holiday schedule are published each year on the school's website and on the main office bulletin board. Major breaks include winter break, spring break, and a summer recess.

Daily Bell Schedule:
Classes typically begin at 8:30 AM and end at 3:00 PM. A full bell schedule (period start/end times) is posted each term and available on the school site.

Enrollment Deadlines:
Application and enrollment deadlines vary by grade and program; contact the admissions office or check the website for current dates.

Parent-Teacher Conferences:
Parent-teacher conferences are held twice per year; sign-ups are managed through the parent portal or by contacting your child’s teacher.

IT & Technical Support:
For help with school accounts, passwords, or the student portal, contact IT support at itsupport@school.edu or call +1-555-151-0099.

Lost and Found:
Lost items are held in the main office for 30 days. Label personal items to speed up returns.

Parking Permits & Drop-off:
Staff and student parking require permits issued by the main office. Morning drop-off is at the front loop; do not block fire lanes.

Visitor Check-In:
All visitors must check in at the main office and wear a visitor badge while on campus.

Immunization Requirements:
All students must provide up-to-date immunization records to the school nurse as required by state law.

Homework Policy:
Homework expectations vary by grade. Teachers publish weekly assignments via the classroom learning platform.

Attendance & Tardiness:
Students are expected to attend daily. To report an absence, call the attendance line or submit the absence form on the website. Tardiness procedures are outlined in the student handbook.

Exam & Assessment Schedules:
Midterm and final exam dates are posted each term. Accommodations for assessments must be arranged through the guidance office.

Special Education & 504 Services:
Support services and Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) are coordinated through the special education office. Contact the special education coordinator for referrals.

Cafeteria Accounts & Payments:
Cafeteria accounts are managed online; parents can add funds via the lunch portal. Free/reduced lunch applications are available from the finance office.

School Nurse & Health Services:
The school nurse is available during school hours for first aid and health concerns. For routine medication at school, submit the medication authorization form.

Code of Conduct & Discipline:
The student code of conduct and discipline policies are in the student handbook and on the website.

Technology Acceptable Use:
Students must follow the school's acceptable use policy when using school devices and networks.

Grade Reporting & Transcripts:
Grades are posted in the parent/student portal. Transcript requests for graduates are handled by the registrar's office.

Graduation & Ceremonies:
Graduation dates and ceremony details are announced in the senior year calendar and via email to families.

Bullying & Reporting Concerns:
To report bullying or safety concerns, contact the principal or use the anonymous reporting form available on the website.

Weather-related Closures & Delays:
School closure and delay announcements are posted on the school website, sent via email/text alerts, and broadcast on local radio.

After-school Care & Enrichment:
Before- and after-school care programs are available for enrolled families. Registration information is available through the activities coordinator.

Contact Directory:
Department contact lists (administration, counselors, transportation, athletics) are available on the website and in the student handbook.
